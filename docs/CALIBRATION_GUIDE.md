# Calibration guide

Use the longest clearly visible straight edge whose real length is known. Place its endpoints on the physical corners, enter the length and unit, then apply calibration. Prefer a reference rectangle in the same plane as the measured face; perspective makes unrelated planes incomparable.

Rectification can prefill proportions, but all template-required values stay inferred until confirmed. Recheck calibration after changing crop, corner placement, or perspective guides. Avoid wide-angle distortion, rolling-shutter skew, soft focus, specular highlights, crushed cartons, and edges hidden by hands or tape.

For critical work, verify the exported 1:1 SVG with a ruler after printing at 100% and test a physical white sample using the intended substrate and crease allowance.
